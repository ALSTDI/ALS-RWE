# Slide Decks and Kits

Every slide deck and handout in the program is listed here by session. The decks are PowerPoint files, so each link downloads the file; GitHub and this site do not display slides in the browser. They are plain black-and-white slides on standard layouts, and you can apply your own theme from the Design tab in PowerPoint.

Day 4 (data extraction) is site specific and has no deck. Build one from the [plain template](templates/Plain-Template.pptx) if your group needs it.
